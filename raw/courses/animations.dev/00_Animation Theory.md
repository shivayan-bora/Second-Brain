## What makes an animation feel right?

- Animations feel right when:
  - they feel natural
  - they have a purpose
  - they are made with taste
- An animation feels right when it mirrors the physics we experience every day. It feels right when you are not surprised by the way it animates, because it feels familiar.

> "It’s not so much that resembling nature is intrinsically good as that nature has had a long time to work on the problem."
>
> - Paul Graham, Taste for Makers

- We can make our animations feel more natural through careful choices: the right easing, duration, and the properties we want to animate. Each decision shapes how the animation feels.

### Purpose of an Animation

- Just because we can add animations to improve the [[User Experience (UX)]], doesn't mean we animate everything on the screen.
- The key is to pace animations through the experience. The more animations we add, the less valuable it becomes.
  - Usually whenever a User is using your product, they don't want to be delighted, they want to achieve their goal.
  - Think about what the user wants to achieve and how often will they see an animation.
    - A hover effect might look nice in a demo, but if the user hovers over the item 50 times a day, even at `200ms` animations start feeling sluggish and creates friction.
